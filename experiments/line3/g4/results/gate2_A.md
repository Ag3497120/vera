## Definitions

- **closed** = the cross hit its first budget collapse (`max_class` 1000, `max_states` 20000, `max_moves` 400000 at level mid) and stays closed for good (N-05). **closed at birth** = `closed_at == opened_at` (the one-shot build of the unit's cross collapsed); **closed later** = closed while being grown, at a step after its birth.
- **cum closed share** = closed crosses / all crosses of the prefix (cumulative, at the end of the step); **at-birth share** = crosses closed at birth / all crosses.
- **grown** = open crosses whose seed is in the document and which had a candidate to seat (`grown[]` of the ledger); **fills** = units seated into them (充填); **narrowed** = crosses with at least one member of the old class without a descendant (絞り); **dropped** = such members, split by `dropped_split` (L-G4-70) into *new* (dropped by the new units) and *recount* (the member's key fell behind under the new counts before any unit was seated).
- **frozen share** (the plan does not define it; this report defines two, both per step): (1) `frozen_share` = of the crosses that existed before the step, the share whose `arr_sha` did not change in the step (no 充填, no 絞り); (2) `frozen seats` = for the crosses grown in the step, the units seated before the step / the units seated after it (the share of the seats the step had to keep). A closed cross is frozen for good; the closed share is a separate column.
- CPU per step = getrusage (self + children) delta; wall = the `extend` call (space rebuild, adhesion, the three tiers one after the other, the snapshot is not in it).
