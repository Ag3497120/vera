# Semantic realization prototype — 2026-10-02

## Status

This is a prototype, not an adopted runtime capability and not a sealed result. It uses no LLM or learned model. Generated text is marked as generated, cites its source clause IDs and spans, and is never written to an evidence store or counted as evidence.

The implementation accepts only sentences whose fresh `semantic_reader.document_view({'gen': text})` read has exactly one supported clause, no unread material, and a projection equal to the input clause. A second check uses the repository morphological tagger without calling the reader: every emitted content lemma must occur in the source clause span, and all other tokens must belong to the closed particle, copula/auxiliary, or punctuation set. A failure in either check produces a typed refusal.

## Closed v1 rule set

| Rule | Emitted form | Preserved fields |
|---|---|---|
| `frame` | Source role terms plus case particles and `realize.conjugate` verb form | Predicate, agent/patient/recipient/origin/location roles, polarity, modality, past/nonpast |
| `copula` identity | `XはYだ/である/です`; plain negative `XはYではない` | Entity/value roles, polarity and asserted modality |
| `copula` property | `XのAはYだ/である/です`; adjective values keep their source form | Entity/attribute/value roles, polarity and asserted modality |
| `measure` | `labelはvalue-unitだ/です`, retaining an optional source substance phrase | Measure predicate and typed entity/label/value/substance roles |

Role terms come from their source `Span.text`; in particular, the property role normalized as `nominal:高い` uses the source surface `高さ`. The verified frame variants include plain and polite conjugation, agent `は` and `が`, and reversal of the non-agent role order when at least two such roles are present. Each surface is checked independently.

Conditions, condition spans, exceptions, unsupported clauses, non-asserted modalities, unlicensed roles, and unknown conjugations are refused. The current reader cannot verify polite negative copulas (`ではありません`) or a negative adjective property without changing the reader/checker contract; those surfaces are refused after round-trip mismatch or role refusal. The reader also interprets some nominal/value surfaces as frame clauses instead of copulas. No gate was relaxed to make those cases pass.

`realize_answer` reads only the source clauses attached to a verified public `Vera.ask` proof. Direct answers may be realized from those clauses. For `Sum` and `Compare`, it retains the existing numeric answer text and returns `NOT_REALIZABLE`; it does not invent comparative prose. Refusals quote exact unread or conflicting source spans when the ask result or semantic view exposes them. If no blocker span exists, no refusal sentence is emitted.

Entity summaries select exact-subject, supported, unguarded clauses in document order, refuse opposite-polarity duplicates with both clause IDs, and deduplicate identical projections while retaining every cited span. Distinct clauses remain separate because a combined surface did not pass the one-clause round trip and closed lineage checks.

## Real-text measurement

`tools/realize_measure.py` used the Wikipedia-lead loader shape from `tools/round5a_route_tune.py`, filtered to `split == train`, with `n=10000` and `stride=100`. It selected 10,000 train documents, parsed 28,236 clauses (5,391 unread segments), and measured each of the 4,713 supported, unguarded clauses in both styles: 9,426 clause/style attempts. Raw rows are in `results/realize/realize_measure_2026-10-02.jsonl`; the aggregate table below is reproduced from the script output.

Counts below are clause/style attempts, so each eligible clause contributes up to two attempts. “Failed a only” means the round-trip check failed and lineage passed; “failed b only” is the inverse. The final column counts cases where both checks failed. Pre-check typed refusals appear in the refusal column but do not count as failed checks.

| Rule | Attempts | Realizable / passed both | Refused by reason | Failed a only | Failed b only | Failed both |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `frame` | 2,140 | 505 | `ROLE_NOT_REALIZABLE` 1,490; `ROUNDTRIP_MISMATCH` 129; `TERM_LINEAGE_MISMATCH` 16 | 118 | 16 | 11 |
| `copula` | 7,236 | 4,731 | `TERM_LINEAGE_MISMATCH` 2,447; `ROUNDTRIP_MISMATCH` 40; `INVALID_PROVENANCE` 14; `ROLE_NOT_REALIZABLE` 4 | 0 | 2,447 | 40 |
| `measure` | 50 | 44 | `TERM_LINEAGE_MISMATCH` 6 | 0 | 6 | 0 |

Frame refusals mostly came from source frames without a supported agent/case-role combination (1,490 attempts); round-trip refusals include ambiguous or otherwise unsupported generated frames. Copula lineage refusals retain tokens outside the closed lineage set, including unlicensed symbols and content not represented by the source roles. The 14 invalid copula provenance cases were reader spans outside their source clause boundary. All such sentences remain refused.

### Meaning-changing mutation audit

The script used 300 distinct successful frame clause/style surfaces and applied one mutation to each. It counted a mutation only when an independent read produced one supported clause with a projection different from the source. Both checks ran independently on every mutated sentence.

| Mutation | N | Round trip catches | Lineage catches | Joint catches |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Flip polarity | 75 | 75 (100%) | 1 (1.3%) | 75 (100%) |
| Swap tense | 75 | 75 (100%) | 0 (0%) | 75 (100%) |
| Swap two role terms | 75 | 75 (100%) | 2 (2.7%) | 75 (100%) |
| Replace a term with one from another document | 75 | 75 (100%) | 75 (100%) | 75 (100%) |
| **Total** | **300** | **300 (100%)** | **78 (26%)** | **300 (100%)** |

The joint catch rate is 100%, above the 99% audit threshold. The lineage check independently catches all cross-document term replacements in this sample; tense, polarity, and role swaps are principally caught by round trip, as intended. These are development measurements on public train leads, not a generalization or adoption claim.

## Not built

- Realization of guards, conditions, or exceptions.
- Multi-sentence cohesion beyond one connector; the attempted connector is kept only if both checks pass, and the current closed rules leave distinct summary clauses separate.
- Free paraphrase beyond the verified style, topic/subject, and role-order variants.
- Generation outside `frame`, `copula`, and `measure` clauses.
- Comparative Japanese prose for `Compare` results.
- General text generation, persistent storage, or conversion of generated output into evidence.
